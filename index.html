About Sharon

I am a Phoenix-based health data scientist and research analyst working extensively with large EHR datasets, statistical modeling, predictive analytics, and modern data tools. My research focuses on outcomes in diabetes and CGM adoption using advanced methods such as survival analysis, causal inference, generalized linear models, mixed models, and longitudinal modeling.

I primarily work in R and SQL (Snowflake, Databricks, VA CDW), and I have growing experience with Python for data manipulation, visualization, and introductory machine learning workflows. I am passionate about applying statistical and ML techniques to real-world data to uncover meaningful trends and support better clinical and operational decisions.
